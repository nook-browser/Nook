# Nook Trademark Policy

Nook's code is free software under [GPL-3.0](./LICENSE). Its name and identity
are not part of that grant.

"Nook", the Nook wordmark, and the Nook app icon are trademarks of Bain Gurley.
Copyright licenses do not convey trademark rights, and GPL-3.0 section 7(e)
allows this restriction to sit alongside the code license.

## What you may do without asking

Fork Nook, modify it, and distribute your fork. Say truthfully that your work
is derived from Nook, or based on Nook, or a fork of Nook. Refer to Nook by
name in discussion, documentation, reviews, comparisons, and articles. Build
and run Nook for yourself under any name you like.

## What needs permission

Distributing a modified Nook under the name "Nook", or under a name close
enough to be confused with it. Using the Nook wordmark or icon on your fork,
its listing, its website, or its marketing. Naming a product, service, or
company in a way that suggests it is Nook or is endorsed by Nook.

Rename your fork and use your own icon. That is the whole requirement.

## Why

GPL-3.0 permits selling copies, and that is deliberate. What the license
cannot stop is someone shipping a rebranded Nook under Nook's own name, so
that its bugs, its data handling, and its support burden land on this project.
Holding the name is what separates a fork from an impersonation.
