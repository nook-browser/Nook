# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Nook has a new maintainer and a new home at nook-browser/Nook. The bundle identifier changed to com.gstudios.nook, so the updater in 1.0.x cannot install this version. Download the DMG, replace Nook in Applications and launch it. The first launch copies your data from the old install.

Requires macOS 26 on Apple Silicon.

- Redesigned sidebar, and a Settings window laid out like System Settings.
- Spaces have accent colors, and each space keeps its own cookies and logins.
- Ad blocking runs on a new engine with uBlock Origin's scriptlets, which covers YouTube ads. Facebook and Instagram feed ads are removed before the page draws them.
- Tab Organizer groups and names your loose tabs with Apple Intelligence.
- Split view, and a video keeps playing in the sidebar when you leave its tab.
- Middle click opens a link in a new tab, or closes a tab in the sidebar.
- Tweaks for YouTube (hide Shorts, frame thumbnails, SponsorBlock) and Facebook (hide Reels and suggested posts), a download button for photos and videos on sites you choose, and Air Traffic Control rules that send a domain to a space.
- Inactive tabs unload to a budget sized to your Mac's memory.
- Private windows leave no favicon, zoom or suggestion traces. Basic auth, camera and microphone prompts, and clipboard access were tightened.
- Passkeys work through a password manager extension such as Bitwarden or 1Password. iCloud Keychain passkeys are unavailable.
